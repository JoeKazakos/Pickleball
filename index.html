Form over time

Ridge regression (α = 0.5) on score differential, refitted on a sliding window. A player’s value is the points per game they add to their team, so a matchup’s expected margin is one side’s two values minus the other’s.

Game by game

Every step on the line has two causes, and both are listed. The top row is what adding that game did. The out row is the game that fell off the back of the window at the same moment — which is how someone who did not play can still move. The two add up to the step exactly. Hover the chart and the matching game lights up.
